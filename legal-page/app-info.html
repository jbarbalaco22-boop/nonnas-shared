About This Integration

This QuickBooks Online integration is an internal automation tool built and used solely by Nonna's Olive Oil. It has no customer-facing web interface, no public launch page, and no self-service connect/disconnect flow — it is not distributed or made available to any third party.

Authorization to QuickBooks Online is performed directly by Nonna's Olive Oil staff via a one-time OAuth authorization step, run locally against our own single QuickBooks Online company. There is no separate "customer" who launches, connects, or disconnects this integration through a web page.
