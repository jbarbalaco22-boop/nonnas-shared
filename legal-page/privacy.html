Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy describes how the internal software integration(s) built by Nonna's Olive Oil ("we", "us") handle data accessed through QuickBooks Online's API.

1. What Data We Access

Read-only access to our own QuickBooks Online company's accounting data, including: profit and loss reports, account balances, and accounts payable (bills). We also read order data from our own Shopify store. No customer-facing personal data (names, addresses, payment details) is intentionally collected or processed beyond what is inherent in standard QuickBooks/Shopify order and accounting records.

2. How We Use This Data

Solely for internal purposes: reconciling revenue across QuickBooks, Shopify, and TikTok Shop, and generating internal daily operational reports. Data is used only by Nonna's Olive Oil and its own systems.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell this data or share it for advertising or marketing purposes. Some data is transmitted to service providers we use to operate these internal tools, specifically: Anthropic's Claude API (to generate a natural-language summary of already-computed financial/operational metrics for an internal daily brief — read-only summarization, not used to make financial decisions or write back to QuickBooks), Google Sheets (to store internal operational reports), and SendGrid (to deliver internal report emails). These providers act solely as processors for Nonna's Olive Oil's own internal use and do not independently use this data for their own purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and credentials are stored locally in environment configuration, not committed to version control, and are not accessible outside Nonna's Olive Oil's own systems. Pulled data is used for report generation and is not retained beyond what is needed for that purpose.

5. Data Retention

We retain only what's needed to generate reconciliation reports and operational reports; we do not build a long-term data warehouse of QuickBooks or Shopify records beyond what's needed for recent-period reporting.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our internal tooling evolves.
